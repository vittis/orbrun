Orbrun - Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup

Orbrun is an unofficial client for Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup (DCSS) with a 2.5D dungeon view. It connects to the public WebTiles servers with the same protocol as the official client, and draws the same game in 2.5D, built to be played on a handheld: with a gamepad on the Steam Deck, or by touch on a phone or tablet.

If you play WebTiles, this is your game, your account, your rc file and your keys. There is nothing to install and no Orbrun account: pick a bundled server, log in or spectate, and play online in the browser.

What it does

  • A 2.5D view of the live dungeon, with flat supporting maps and minimap.
  • Full gamepad support: a contextual A button, command menus, an on-screen keyboard, and an action bar that always says what each button does.
  • Everything the official client shows: menus, prompts, the monster list, the message log, chat, the level map and targeting.
  • Your rc file, read live from the server and never overridden.
  • Loads supported stable and trunk tiles and enums from each server’s own data.
  • Plays on a phone or tablet by touch, in portrait or landscape.
  • Runs from your Steam library on a Steam Deck, or from a phone’s or tablet’s home screen.
