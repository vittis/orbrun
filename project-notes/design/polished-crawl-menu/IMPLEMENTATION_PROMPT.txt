APPROVED DESIGN

1. Name: Polished Crawl. Exact main subtitle: "Classic Crawl, with a 2.5D perspective and enhanced visual effects." Keep this descriptive subtitle under the title. Do not restore generic explanations beneath menu options or a separate hover-help paragraph. Real contextual subtitles ARE required: saved-character details, live-player counts, and genuine loading/error state.

2. Dark full-screen gameplay backdrop; left-aligned title and menu; enough shading behind text to read it. Preserve the reference's overall spacing and emphasis, but use a responsive layout, not its fixed stage dimensions or screenshot-specific crop coordinates. The actual 2.5D dungeon should remain clearly visible on the right. Support narrow screens and touch without clipping, horizontal overflow, or shrinking text to fit.

3. Typography: the reference combines a strong Barlow Condensed title, IBM Plex Sans descriptive/modal copy, and IBM Plex Mono menu commands and compact account controls. POLISHED is smaller, spaced, and muted; CRAWL is large with a warm brass gradient and restrained depth. Keep display typography scoped to the app shell. Do not replace measured gameplay/HUD glyph fonts or alter GridHost metrics as a side effect.

4. Title shine: an angled, narrow reflected-light band sweeps LEFT TO RIGHT across the CRAWL letterforms only, then rests. Reference timing is an eight-second cycle with roughly a 1.3-second sweep after a long quiet interval. It must not flash the whole title, move the layout, or become a constant shimmer. Use a lightweight CSS treatment or equivalent; no new canvas/RAF loop just for the logo. Preserve a readable static brass title when animation is disabled. Honor existing Still settings AND prefers-reduced-motion, respond when either changes, and suspend decorative work when hidden, covered by a modal, or unmounted. Keep the accessible title "Polished Crawl" without announcing duplicate decorative letters.

5. Account area: a thin, unobtrusive WebTiles-style row at the top. Server picker on the left; User, Pass, Log in on the right; small Register and Forgot password links beneath when signed out. Once signed in, show username and Log out. Keep the top area compact, with labels beside inputs where space allows, and adequate touch targets at narrow/coarse-pointer sizes. Use the existing server/account infrastructure, including supported account management and custom-server flows; keep these reachable without adding footer entries. Preserve registration/recovery and reconnect/error handling. Never fake authentication or invent supported recovery endpoints.

6. Main menu: retain the existing leading-symbol and contextual-row idiom. Example only:
   > Play DCSS 0.34                                      (edit rc)
   > Continue DCSS trunk                                (edit rc)
     vittiSXX the Grappler, Troll Fighter XL11
   { Watch
     1 playing
   ? Settings
   Keep edit rc a separate focusable action, not a nested button. Use the real server's offered versions, availability, RC ids, and existing save-detection precedence. Never hardcode the example version, user, character, count, or server. Do not display Continue unless supported by existing confirmed account/save evidence. Distinguish disconnected/loading/unknown from zero or no save. Retain stable row identity/order/focus as lobby data updates.

7. Remove Play Descent and Continue Descent from the redesigned home offerings. Filter these intentionally at the app's game-offering/presentation boundary using verified game identity; do not corrupt protocol parsing or broadly delete shared game-link handling. Preserve ordinary DCSS stable/trunk offerings and the intended handling of other supported DCSS versions.

8. Footer: ONLY About & credits and What's new. No Still control, View gameplay, How to play, account shortcut, or other legacy footer items. Existing motion settings remain available through Settings; removing the footer control does not remove accessibility preferences.

9. About & credits and What's new open accessible MODALS OVER THE MENU, not a new document page. Reuse the actual repository content and credits; the mockup's modal prose is only a layout example. Use one shared modal primitive with separate content, meaningful headings, a close button, Escape/back behavior, focus containment/restoration, background input isolation, and bounded scrolling for long content. Preserve the server/account selection and form state across opening/closing and lobby refreshes. Preserve news-read tracking with its compatible storage key; opening What's new is what marks it read. Support keyboard, gamepad cancel/navigation, touch, and the existing back behavior. Pause background video and decorative shine while covered. Do not redirect menu clicks to /about or /about/new.

10. No server-specific contextual banners, victory/death cards, MOTD, or statistics feed. Keep live Watch information and saved-character context; these are functional menu data, not the rejected server-news feature.
