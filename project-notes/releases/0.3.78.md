# Release 0.3.78 — Body bumps without weapon VFX

Source version **0.3.78**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.75`**. Publication follows the main-triggered Workers Builds path in [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md).

Removed weapon artwork, ribbons/trails, per-weapon gesture/rotation paths, the dedicated melee preview and its raster lane, obsolete VFX settings, and the unused incoming enemy-swing parser/queue. One body-bump clock now serves all melee weapon types. Combat exposes only **Hit bump strength** and **Hit bump snap**; strength Off plants attacker and target. Saved bump tuning and unrelated preferences survive normalization, while retired weapon settings are ignored and disappear on the next settings write.

Ordinary blood/material hit splashes, confirmed damage, auxiliary-attack target matching, spells and death effects remain. Bumps stay bounded and respect visibility, Still/reduced motion and actor identity without changing authoritative cells. The shared combat preview retains bump/splash inspection and offers the two bump controls.

Merged concurrent main **`7e5ab36` / 0.3.77**. Resolved the shared renderer imports, motion call and reset conflicts by retaining its native-spell area suppression, stable cloud positions and cleanup together with the bump-only API. Its spell-presentation helper, lighting changes and expanded native-art raster checks are preserved. No force push, reset or tag replacement is used.
