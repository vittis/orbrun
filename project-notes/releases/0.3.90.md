# Release 0.3.90 — Raised side walls

Source version **0.3.90**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.87`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the automatic main-triggered Workers Build.

Corridor cutaways previously flattened connected wall rows even when they were beside or behind the player. Exposed wall ends next to open ground could consequently appear flat. Gameplay now reserves the cutaway for rows south of the player; side and back walls retain their full raised stonework and shaded vertical faces. Connected rows still share one elevation, and picking, lighting and shadows use that elevation. Narrow passages in front of the player retain their clearance.

Integrated concurrent release **`1047c1b` / 0.3.89**, preserving its wider camera ranges and endpoint checks. This release changes no server state, combat, supporting flat maps, storage or production boundaries. Other worktrees and their running servers were left alone.
