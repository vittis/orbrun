# Release 0.3.83 — Continuous wall corners

Source version **0.3.83**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.80`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the main-triggered Workers Build.

Raised side-wall caps now extend to the start of a known, lower adjoining wall cap. This removes the dark internal face band at foreground corners while retaining the existing elevations, footprint, picking and corridor clearance. Frontier shading follows the continued cap. Missing, unseen or unknown neighbours, open ground and water do not create joins; exposed wall ends keep their faces.

Feature commit `bb2b480` was merged with concurrent release `b0e01d1` through `5406fe0`, preserving Djinni hit-feedback fixes, continuous actor animation and the preceding spell/terrain work. Other worktrees and their occupied browser servers were not modified.

- The new corner raster regression fails in all 54 combinations with the old geometry and passes in all 54 with the fix. Real-art before/after captures were inspected locally. Both corner directions, three display densities, three tilts, three zooms and filtered/unfiltered scaling are covered.
- Unit regression covers remembered/visible joins and rejects joins into missing, unseen, unknown, floor and water cells.

- The full browser gate includes **4,558 dungeon raster assertions**, 80 native-spell checks, 240 spell-terrain checks, 12 atmosphere checks, reduced motion/Still, narrow mouse/touch HUD, map lifecycle and online-only production acceptance. The temporary config changes only the strict development port to 5586; production uses 5287.
- Reviewed the integrated runtime and release metadata diff. Optional engine execution, authenticated play and physical-device acceptance are outside this verification.
