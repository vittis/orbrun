# Release 0.3.88 — Shorter mutual bumps and fine combat controls

Source version **0.3.88**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.85`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the main-triggered Workers Build.

Mutual melee attacks shorten their lunges as the opposing body approaches, reaching about half the ordinary travel at contact. The reduction follows the opposing pose so staggered counterattacks stay continuous. Unrelated attacks and actor identities remain separate.

All combat preferences now offer five-percentage-point steps. Hit bump speed ranges from 50% to 200%, defaulting to 100%. Each confirmed attack captures its speed; lunge, recoil, contact flash, spray onset and motion expiry use that clock. Settings persist and reset with Combat without changing authoritative cells, gameplay timing, Still/reduced motion or the online-only production boundary.

Integrated main **`e92dec2` / 0.3.87**, preserving its finer Dungeon tilt and zoom controls. Settings implementation and regression changes merged cleanly.
