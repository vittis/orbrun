# Release 0.3.84 — Crunchier combat and adjustable gore

Source version **0.3.84**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.81`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the main-triggered Workers Build.

Melee contact arrives at 55 ms (previously 134 ms), with a stronger lunge, fast recoil, brief compression and a 240 ms recovery cycle. The shared contact clock synchronizes hit flashes, material sprays and spell feedback. Hit sprays expire within 300 ms (previously 650 ms) and launch more droplets immediately. Blood uses darker rounded pixel drops, irregular splashes and flesh chunks; bloodless materials retain their own art. Death bursts are denser, launch faster and leave larger splatter.

Separate persisted Hit gore and Death gore settings range from Off to 200%; bump strength also reaches 200%. These remain local preferences. Confirmed damage, visibility clipping, bounded histories, Still/reduced motion, flat supporting maps and the online-only production boundary are preserved.
