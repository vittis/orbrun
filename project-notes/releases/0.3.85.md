# Release 0.3.85 — Spray direction controls and exclusive death effects

Source version **0.3.85**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.82`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the main-triggered Workers Build.

Combat settings add 175% bump strength and 125% hit/death gore. Independent Hit outward spray and Death outward spray preferences choose the share emitted around the impact: 0% follows the blow, 100% spreads around the body. Both default to 35%. Density remains controlled by the separate gore preferences. Persistence validates finite values, clamps each share to 0–100%, preserves existing preferences and keeps combat preferences local.

Confirmed kills establish death ownership before the contact bump can emit particles. The app omits lethal hit flashes, impact bursts and spell-result bursts; the renderer cancels pending hit effects and suppresses delayed reports for the deceased actor, including with death gore Off. A bounded, visibility-aware history replaces the old cell-only temporary hiding rule, so another actor entering the same cell still receives hit feedback. This never predicts a death or delays input. Native projectile artwork remains server-owned.
