# Release 0.3.92 — Readable miss and zero-damage symbols

Source version **0.3.92**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.89`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the automatic main-triggered Workers Build.

The previous contact cues were too faint during play. Misses now use a pale-blue outlined X with trailing whiff marks, making the failed strike identifiable in a single frame. Explicit zero-damage hits show a gold shield-shaped contact flash with three short sparks. Larger silhouettes, brighter shaded colour ramps and increased display scale make both cues more pronounced. Misses remain visible for 420 ms and harmless contacts for 360 ms, with the existing dithered fade.

Contact onset, confirmed server outcomes, damage precedence, actor tracking, visibility/death cleanup, Still/reduced motion and the online-only boundary are preserved. Neither cue emits blood or floor residue. The prior camera and wall releases remain in the source history; no other worktrees or servers were changed.
