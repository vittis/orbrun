# Release 0.3.98 — Material defensive combat VFX

Source version **0.3.98**, immutable source tag **`cloudflare-online-v1.95`**. Publication follows [DEPLOYMENT.md](../DEPLOYMENT.md) through the automatic main-triggered Workers Build.

Defensive feedback now uses material effects without icons: curved wind streaks for dodges, scattered steel flecks for zero-damage armor impacts, and a golden ricochet fan for shield blocks. Each burst expands and settles around the recipient's body, continues moving throughout its smooth fade, and uses a distinct trajectory for simultaneous attempts.

The existing independent accumulation remains intact: repeated outcomes and simultaneous shield/dodge/armor feedback coexist with blood, bounded to six defensive bursts per recipient and 24 globally. Confirmed combat timing, visibility, death/removal cleanup, Still and reduced motion remain enforced. This release builds on 0.3.97 and preserves the intervening quick-settings and attacker-attribution work; remote main was unchanged during preparation.
